Jarvisettin Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Jarvisettin is a private desktop tool used by one person, its owner, to manage their own channels. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what it does with account data.

What it does with an account

When the owner connects one of their own accounts, Jarvisettin reads that account's public profile details, statistics and analytics, and its list of recent posts. On YouTube it uses the permissions youtube.force-ssl and yt-analytics.readonly. With these, and only when the owner asks it to, it uploads a video to the owner's channel, sets its title, description, tags, thumbnail, visibility and publishing time, and changes those details on the owner's existing videos. It does not delete videos. It does not publish anything on its own. On Instagram and TikTok it only reads.

Where it is kept

Account data stays on the owner's own computer. Login tokens are stored encrypted. There is no Jarvisettin server, and no account data is sent to the developer. The only things that leave the computer are the videos and details the owner chooses to publish, which go directly to YouTube.

How it is used

The data is shown to the owner so they can manage their channels and follow how they perform. It is not used for advertising.

Sharing

Account data is not sold and is not shared with anyone.

Removing access and data

Choosing "disconnect" in the app deletes the login and the data read through it from the owner's computer. Access can also be removed at any time on the Google security settings page.

Google and YouTube

Jarvisettin uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account, the owner also agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. Jarvisettin's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page. Questions can be sent through this project's GitHub issues page.
