Jarvisettin

Jarvisettin is a private desktop tool that one person uses to manage their own video channels. It helps plan and produce videos, and it shows the owner the statistics of their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts in one place.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up or use it, and it only reads accounts the owner connects themselves. Jarvisettin only reads those accounts; it does not post, edit or delete anything on them.
