Jarvisettin

Jarvisettin is a private desktop tool that one person uses to manage their own video channels. It helps plan and produce videos, publish them to the owner's own YouTube channel, and see the statistics of the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts in one place.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up or use it, and it only works with accounts the owner connects themselves. Jarvisettin uploads or changes a video only when the owner asks it to.
